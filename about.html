Weaving trust, thread by thread

A carpet manufacturing and trading house built on traditional craft, considered design and long-term relationships with the people who make and buy our carpets.

Company Overview

Manufacturers and traders under one roof

We bring two businesses together: a manufacturing operation that designs and weaves carpets to order, and a trading arm that sources finished collections from trusted weaving partners.

That combination lets us serve very different clients well. An interior designer can commission a one-off silk rug; a hotel group can specify thousands of square metres of tufted carpet; a retailer can build a seasonal range of flatweaves and jute rugs. All of them work with the same team, the same standards and a single point of contact.

Carpet Craftsmanship

Skills passed down through generations

Our weavers come from families who have worked the loom for generations.

Their knowledge of how tightly to pack a row, how to read a design graph and how a colour will shift after washing cannot be automated, and it shows in every carpet we make.

  • Hand-knotting, hand-tufting and handloom weaving under one roof
  • Design graphs and CAD prepared by our in-house studio
  • Fair wages and safe, well-lit workspaces for every artisan
  • Ongoing training that keeps traditional skills alive

Quality & Material Sourcing

It begins with the right fibre

We buy from established suppliers and test every lot before it reaches the loom. Each batch of yarn is dye-tested for colourfastness and matched against the approved colour standard.

Wool

Indian highland and imported wools, selected for staple length, lustre and resilience, then carded and spun into consistent, even yarn.

Silk, Viscose & Blends

Silk and plant-based lustre fibres are tested for strength, sheen and colourfastness before they are blended or woven.

Jute, Sisal & Cotton

Natural fibres from established growing regions. Strong cotton forms the warp and weft foundation of our hand-knotted rugs.

Manufacturing Process

Six stages, one standard

Every carpet follows the same carefully managed journey from raw fibre to delivery, whether it is a single rug or a contract order.

Explore the full process

  1. Material Selection

    Fibre graded and tested before acceptance.

  2. Yarn Preparation

    Carded, spun and small-batch dyed.

  3. Weaving

    Knotted, tufted or woven by hand.

  4. Finishing

    Washed, sheared, carved and bound.

  5. Quality Inspection

    Checked against the approved specification.

  6. Final Delivery

    Protectively packed and shipped worldwide.

Design Philosophy

"We design carpets to be lived on: rooted in tradition, edited for today, and made to outlast the trends around them."

Our studio draws on centuries of Persian, Indian and tribal design as well as contemporary art, architecture and nature. Every design is drawn at full scale, coloured with real yarn poms and refined until it feels right in the room it is made for.
