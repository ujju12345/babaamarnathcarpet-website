Our Story

A heritage of craft, woven for modern living

Baba Amarnath Carpet & Rugs is guided by a simple belief: a carpet should be made with as much care as the home it lives in.

Today we are manufacturers and traders of handcrafted carpets, working with skilled artisan families across India's carpet-weaving belt. We design in-house, source fibres with care and oversee every stage from dyeing to the final wash, supplying homeowners, interior designers, hotels and export partners worldwide.

Craftsmanship

Every knot, tied by hand

Our carpets are made the slow way. Master weavers translate each design knot by knot and row by row, on looms that have been part of India's carpet heritage for centuries.

  1. Hand Knotted

    The finest construction: individual knots tied on a vertical loom, at densities of up to 300+ knots per square inch.

  2. Hand Tufted

    Yarn punched through canvas with a hand-held tool, then sheared and finished. Ideal for bold, bespoke designs.

  3. Hand Woven

    Warp and weft interlaced on traditional handlooms for flatweaves, dhurries and textured loop carpets.
