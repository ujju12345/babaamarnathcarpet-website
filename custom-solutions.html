Carpets made for your project

Custom sizes, designs and colours for residences, hotels and workplaces, developed with you and for the way your space will be used.

Made to Measure

Everything can be customised

Start from one of our collections and adapt it, or begin with a blank page. Either way, you approve every detail before weaving begins.

01

Custom Sizes & Shapes

Any dimension, from small accent rugs to seamless room-size carpets and long corridor runners. Rounds, ovals, irregular and freeform shapes are cut and bound by hand.

02

Custom Designs

Choose from our archive of traditional and contemporary designs, adapt an existing pattern, or share your own artwork. Our studio prepares CAD renders and full-scale design graphs.

03

Custom Colours

Colours are matched to Pantone or NCS references, paint chips or fabric swatches. You approve physical yarn poms and a strike-off sample before production begins.

Sectors We Serve

Residential, hospitality & commercial

Residential Carpets

Statement rugs for living and dining rooms, soft bedroom carpets and runners for stairs and hallways, sized to your floor plan.

  • Room-size and wall-to-wall carpets
  • Stair and hallway runners
  • Heirloom hand-knotted pieces

Hospitality Carpets

Lobbies, corridors, guest rooms, ballrooms and restaurants. Contract-grade carpets that carry your brand palette and stand up to heavy footfall.

  • Corridor and guest-room carpets
  • Lobby and ballroom feature rugs
  • Fire-safety specifications on request

Commercial Carpets

Offices, retail, auditoriums and institutions. Durable broadloom, carpet tiles and logo rugs that balance performance with design.

  • Carpet tiles and broadloom
  • Logo and wayfinding rugs
  • Acoustic and underfoot comfort

Trade Collaborations

Partners to designers & architects

We work as an extension of your practice, translating concepts into carpets and managing production, so you can focus on the design of the whole space.

Interior Designer Collaborations

From mood board to installed rug, we help you present options to your clients with confidence and deliver exactly what was promised.

  • Trade pricing and a dedicated account manager
  • Sample library, yarn poms and colour cards
  • Client-ready CAD renders and room visualisations
  • Private-label and exclusive designs

Architect Collaborations

For architectural and contract projects we supply the technical detail and coordinated schedules that fit your programme.

  • Technical specifications and performance data
  • BOQ and tender support
  • Large-volume and phased deliveries
  • Site measurement and installation coordination

How It Works

From brief to installation

  1. Brief

    Share your space, drawings, inspiration, quantities and budget.

  2. Design & CAD

    Our studio prepares designs and renders for review.

  3. Samples

    Approve yarn poms, colour cards and a strike-off.

  4. Production

    Your carpet is made by hand with progress updates.

  5. Quality Check

    Measured and inspected against the approved sample.

  6. Delivery

    Packed, shipped and coordinated with your installer.
